# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (unreleased)

First public version.

**Software**
- Live NFL gamecast: field view (TV tilt or flat) and scoreboard view, animations for scoring plays, turnovers, first
  downs, red zone, kickoff, halftime and final
- Scores by division (4 per screen, with byes), standings by division, conference or playoff seed
- Favorites, hidden teams, game locking, TV sync delay
- Touch controls: tap bar, hold for quick settings with a QR code to the phone settings page
- Wi-Fi setup on the screen with an on-screen keyboard and automatic rollback; *No Wi-Fi* screen
- Self-installing DietPi SD card script; touch fix for multi-touch panels; hidden cursor

**Frame**
- Field frame for the Hosyond 7" (tested) and Waveshare 7.9" (untested)
- Back tray with a labeled spot for every part, print-in-place kickstand with click-shut catch, wall keyholes
- Snap-in end zones with flush two-color lettering
- OpenSCAD customizers: end zones; back tray (board presets, board rotation, power and switch holes, kickstand on/off)
