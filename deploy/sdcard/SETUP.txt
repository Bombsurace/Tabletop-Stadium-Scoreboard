TABLETOP STADIUM SCOREBOARD - SD CARD THAT SETS ITSELF UP
=========================================
Full guide with pictures: docs/software-setup.md

1. Download DietPi for "Orange Pi Zero 2W" from dietpi.com and write it to the
   card with balenaEtcher or Raspberry Pi Imager. Unplug the card and plug it
   back in so your computer shows the small boot drive.

2. Copy Automation_Custom_Script.sh (from the latest GitHub Release) onto that
   drive, next to dietpi.txt.
   Optional: open it in a text editor and paste a Tailscale auth key between the
   quotes on the TAILSCALE_AUTHKEY line. Change nothing else in it.

3. Open dietpi-wifi.txt and set your Wi-Fi name and password:
      aWIFI_SSID[0]='YourWiFiName'
      aWIFI_KEY[0]='YourWiFiPassword'

4. Open dietpi.txt, find each line below (Ctrl+F) and change it to this value.
   Use your own password and time zone.
      AUTO_SETUP_AUTOMATED=1
      AUTO_SETUP_GLOBAL_PASSWORD=pick-a-password
      AUTO_SETUP_NET_WIFI_ENABLED=1
      AUTO_SETUP_NET_WIFI_COUNTRY_CODE=US
      AUTO_SETUP_TIMEZONE=America/Los_Angeles
      AUTO_SETUP_NET_HOSTNAME=scoreboard
      AUTO_SETUP_SSH_SERVER_INDEX=-2
      AUTO_SETUP_AUTOSTART_TARGET_INDEX=7
      AUTO_SETUP_AUTOSTART_LOGIN_USER=dietpi
      SURVEY_OPTED_IN=0
      CONFIG_SERIAL_CONSOLE_ENABLE=0
   Save, eject, and put the card in the Orange Pi.

5. Plug it in and leave it 15-20 minutes. It updates, installs everything and
   restarts by itself. If the screen sits at a text login prompt after 20
   minutes, unplug it and plug it back in once.

6. It comes up on the scoreboard. Press and hold the screen for 1 second: the
   menu shows the board's address and a QR code for the settings page on your
   phone.

Log in from a computer:  ssh dietpi@<address from the menu>   (dietpi@scoreboard works on some networks)
Setup log on the board:  cat /var/tmp/dietpi/logs/scoreboard-setup.log
